📎 The Stapler That Pins Time

Freeze a moving ball with a well-timed staple. When time resumes, the pin snaps it toward the golden goal!

Level 1Score 0Staples 3Time 30sBest 0

Tap the moving ball (or press SPACE) to pin time. Release launches it toward the goal!

How to play: the blue ball bounces around. Tap the ball to freeze it for up to 2 seconds; while frozen, drag away from the ball to aim the rebound arrow, then release. A successful shot into the golden hoop earns points and a new level. Each miss costs a staple. Touch, mouse and keyboard work. SPACE pins the ball anywhere; LEFT/RIGHT adjust aim while frozen; ENTER releases. Restart resets everything.
