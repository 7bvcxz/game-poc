Tap an occupied square to fold/unfold it. Folded buildings stop earning rent, but pack smaller. Tap again to unfold.

The suitcase has a packing limit. Houses cost 3 space unfolded / 1 folded; parks cost 2 / 1. Your moving bonus grows with residents and the number of buildings you successfully pack.
